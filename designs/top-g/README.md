- The base model and variants share the gray blazer, black crewneck, silver aviators, and head mapped to the portrait. The idle animation adapts the photo’s fingertip pose. The lapel pin and victory guard refer to chess and kickboxing. [Andrew Tate interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NfOWwoFuWZ8)

The finish also appears on the Splash racer, tank armor, mounted gear, mallet, barrel bands and pilot controls.
